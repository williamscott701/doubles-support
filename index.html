Doubles

The sliding number puzzle, carved in wood, for iPhone and iPad. A path of 120 levels, a new challenge every day, and free play to 4096. No ads, no tracking, no account.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to williamscott701@gmail.com. Please include your device and iOS version if something went wrong.

What's in the game

  • The path: 120 levels in twelve woods, from birch to ebony. Each level asks for one tile within a number of moves; fewer moves earn more stars. Knots, small trays and placed tiles arrive as you climb.
  • A daily challenge: a new puzzle every day, the same for everyone on that date, with a streak.
  • Fair by design: a level's tiles come the same way every time and for everyone, and every level has been finished within its limit by a computer player that cannot see the tiles to come.
  • Free play on three boards: 3×3 Tight (plays to 512), 4×4 Classic and 5×5 Relaxed (play to 4096, with 2048 marked on the way).
  • Today's board: the same starting tiles and the same new tiles for everyone on the same date.
  • Unlimited undo, which you can switch off. Games with undo are counted as assisted runs, apart from pure runs.
  • Best score and best tile per board, total merges, games played, a daily streak, and recent games.
  • A summary at the end of every game, with its growth rings and a full move-by-move replay.
  • Swipes with an adjustable distance, arrow keys and W A S D on a keyboard, haptics, optional sounds, birch and walnut finishes, VoiceOver and Dynamic Type.

The trial and the plans

Your first 10 days are the full game, free. You start them yourself from the first screen with Start 10-day Trial; it costs nothing and never turns into a charge on its own.

After 10 days the whole game locks until you choose a plan. Your levels, stars, streak and saved games are kept:

  • Workshop Monthly: US$0.99 a month, renews automatically.
  • Workshop Yearly: US$4.99 a year, renews automatically.
  • Workshop for Good: US$7.99 once, no renewal, with Family Sharing.

Prices in other countries are shown in your App Store and in the app before you buy. There are no ads. The full terms are in the Terms of Use.

Restoring and managing

On a new device, open Doubles and tap Restore Purchases on the first screen, on the plan screen, or in Settings under Doubles Workshop. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. To cancel a subscription, use Manage Subscription in Doubles' Settings, or Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device.
