Doubles

The sliding number puzzle, carved in wood, for iPhone and iPad. Merge matching tiles all the way to 4096. No ads, no tracking, no account.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: write to williamscott701@gmail.com. Please include your device and iOS version if something went wrong.

What's in the game

  • Three boards: 3×3 Tight (plays to 512), 4×4 Classic and 5×5 Relaxed (play to 4096, with 2048 marked on the way).
  • Today's board: the same starting tiles and the same new tiles for everyone on the same date.
  • Unlimited undo, which you can switch off. Games with undo are counted as assisted runs, apart from pure runs.
  • Best score and best tile per board, total merges, games played, a daily streak, and recent games.
  • A summary at the end of every game, with its growth rings and a full move-by-move replay.
  • Swipes with an adjustable distance, arrow keys and W A S D on a keyboard, haptics, optional sounds, birch and walnut finishes, VoiceOver and Dynamic Type.

Free

All of Doubles is free: every board, the daily board and replays. There are no ads, no purchases and no subscription.
