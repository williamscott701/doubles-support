Privacy Policy

Doubles collects no data. Last updated 6 October 2026.

What Doubles collects

Nothing. Doubles has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that could do any of these. The developer receives no information about you or how you play.

What stays on your device

Your games in progress, your stats, your settings and a note of whether the trial has started or the full game is owned are saved on your device only, so the game can pick up where you left off. They are removed if you delete the app. Device backups you make (for example to iCloud) may include them, under Apple's own privacy terms.

Network use

The only network traffic is Apple's StoreKit, which Doubles uses to show the price, start the trial, sell the full game and restore purchases. Those transactions are processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; Doubles receives only whether a purchase is valid.

Children

Doubles is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
