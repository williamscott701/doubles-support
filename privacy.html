Privacy Policy

Anonymous usage statistics only, never linked to you, and you can turn them off. No account, no ads, no tracking. Last updated 12 November 2026.

What Doubles collects

Doubles counts anonymous usage statistics with Google Analytics for Firebase, so the developer can see how the game is used and make it better: for example which levels are cleared and in how many moves, which boards get played, how often the daily challenge is finished, and how many people start the trial or choose a plan.

These statistics are not linked to you. Doubles sends no name, email, account or user ID, does not collect the advertising identifier (the part of the SDK that reads it is not included) or the vendor identifier, and sends no advertising signals. Google may derive an approximate country from the connection, as any website can.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics storage stays switched off, so only cookieless, identifier-free measurements are sent.

Turning it off

In Doubles, open Settings and turn off Share anonymous play counts. Nothing more is sent from then on, including on later launches. It is on until you turn it off.

No tracking, no ads

Doubles does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, shows no ads, and has no account or sign-in. It does not sell or share any data for advertising.

What stays on your device

Your games in progress, your levels, stars and streak, your stats and your settings are saved on your device only, so the game can pick up where you left off. They are removed if you delete the app. Device backups you make (for example to iCloud) may include them, under Apple's own privacy terms.

Reminders and share cards

The daily reminder is off until you turn it on. It is a notification scheduled on your device by Doubles itself; nothing is sent to a server to deliver it. iOS asks for notification permission only when you choose "Remind me" or turn on the reminder in Settings.

Share cards are images Doubles draws on your device from your result. They go only where you send them with the iOS share sheet. If you tap Save to Photos, iOS asks once whether Doubles may add photos; that permission lets Doubles add the cards you save, and never lets it see your library.

Purchases

When you start the trial, buy a plan or restore purchases, the purchase goes through Apple's App Store under Apple's privacy terms. The developer receives no personal information from it; the anonymous statistics only note that a trial or plan was started.

Who processes the statistics

Google, as the provider of Google Analytics for Firebase, under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer sees only aggregated reports.

Children

Doubles is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children; the anonymous statistics described above can be turned off in Settings.
